# Consult response rubric

A high-value consultation should satisfy most of the following.

## Specificity

- Names exact mechanisms, symbols, files, assumptions, or failure modes.
- Explains consequences rather than listing style preferences.
- Distinguishes primary defects from optional cleanup.

## Independence

- Does not merely mirror the current approach.
- States disagreement where evidence supports it.
- Considers at least one plausible alternative explanation or design.

## Epistemic quality

- Separates facts, inference, and speculation.
- Identifies missing evidence.
- Recommends discriminating tests or observations.
- Does not invent execution results.

## Actionability

- Produces a decision, ranked findings, minimal patch plan, or concrete next tests.
- Respects constraints and non-goals.
- Avoids broad rewrites without necessity.

## Mode discipline

- Remains advisory.
- Does not take over the user conversation.
- Does not claim ownership of primary edits/tools.

## Reject or re-query when

- the answer mostly restates context;
- recommendations are generic;
- key claims lack mechanisms/evidence;
- it ignores an explicit constraint;
- it assumes unavailable tools/access;
- it confuses Consult with Delegate.
